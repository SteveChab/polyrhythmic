Three free browser apps

Your eyes see rhythms. Your ears hear color.

polyrhythmic.design ties it all together.

Play three beats against four fast enough and your ear hears a chord. The light we can see spans almost exactly one octave, so every note has a color.

How a rhythm becomes a color

  1. Rhythm1.72 beats a seconda slow, steady pulse
  2. arrow_downwarddoubled 8 times
  3. PitchA4, 440 Hza note you can hear
  4. arrow_downwarddoubled 40 more times
  5. Light484 THz, orange-reda color you can see

Every doubling is one octave higher. The beat, the note and the color are all A.
